What is included

Peer-reviewed journal articles, reviews, and three full reviewed proceedings papers: TracPy (2014), From Blowout to Beach (2014), and Ten Years of Realtime, Near-Surface Current Observations Supporting Oil Spill Response (2005). Publication years follow the journal volume year where available, with online publication dates used for recent work. Bibliographic metadata was checked on November 17, 2026.

What is excluded

AGU and EGU meeting abstracts, preprints, datasets, software records without a reviewed paper, editorial and reviewer-thanks notices, EOS forum articles, and technical reports are excluded. Books and book chapters with unconfirmed individual review status are also excluded from this research list. These works remain available in the previous website archive.
